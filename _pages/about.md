I am a bioinformatician and metagenomics microbiologist at the [Centre for Microbiome Research](https://microbiome.qut.edu.au/) (QUT), working in the Woodcroft group. I build and apply tools for genome-resolved metagenomics: [Bin Chicken](https://github.com/AroneyS/binchicken) (targeted co-assembly), [CoverM](https://github.com/wwood/CoverM) (read-mapping statistics), [Aviary](https://github.com/rhysnewell/aviary) (assembly and recovery) and [Galah](https://github.com/wwood/galah) (genome dereplication), and I contribute to [SingleM](https://github.com/wwood/singlem) and its [Sandpiper](https://sandpiper.qut.edu.au/) community-profile resource.

Applied to global and climate-focused datasets, this work has:

- identified more than 140,000 previously undescribed bacterial and archaeal species,
- generated community composition profiles for >750,000 public metagenomes,
- recovered genomes from >7,000 species in Arctic permafrost wetlands (NSF EMERGE), and
- supported studies of plastic degradation in insect guts, the Rhizobium–legume symbiosis and chemotaxis in rhizobia.

My earlier training (BSc/Hons, University of Queensland; DPhil, University of Oxford) was in experimental microbiology, focusing on chemotaxis and motility in *Rhizobium leguminosarum*. I also serve on the QUT School of Biomedical Sciences HDR engagement committee, help administer the Centre's HPC, and sit on the Microbiome Virtual International Forum (MVIF) Pacific organising committee.
